A calmer first step for reporting a problem, before you have to walk into a station.

File what happened in your own words. It reaches the police station nearest to your area, an officer picks it up, and you can watch the status change — from a quiet complaint to a formal case, if it needs to go that far.

How it works

1

You describe the problemGive your area and what happened — no visit required to start. Neethii can help you put it into words.

2

It's routed to your nearest stationMatched to a police station by your district and taluk.

3

An officer takes chargeStatus moves from Pending to Assigned to Under Investigation.

4

Serious matters become a caseEscalated complaints get a case number tied to your record, for the long term.

What this is, and what it isn't

This is a working design concept

Every screen — filing, routing, officer status changes, case escalation, even chatting with Neethii — actually runs, so you can click through the full idea end to end. Data you enter is saved only in this browser, on your device.

This is not the real Tamil Nadu Police

Nothing here reaches an actual station, an actual officer, or Aadhaar's real database. A real version of this would need to be built and operated by the Tamil Nadu Police department itself, with proper identity verification and legal safeguards.

About this concept, and where to get real help

Why this kind of portal is worth designing

Plenty of people hesitate before walking into a police station — out of fear, distance, or simply not knowing how a complaint becomes a case. A well-run digital front door, run by the police department itself with real verification, could lower that first barrier while still routing serious matters to a human officer quickly.

What a real version would need, that this demo doesn't have

  • Operation by Tamil Nadu Police or an authorised government body — not a third party.
  • Aadhaar verification through UIDAI's own authorised process, not a text field.
  • A live link into the real police station and court case-management systems.
  • Legal safeguards: how false complaints are handled, how evidence is preserved, how a citizen appeals a decision.
  • Data protection matching India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.
